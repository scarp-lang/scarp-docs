## Shipped changes

- Pure deterministic `top_p(probabilities, mass)` and `top_p_values`: rank a normalized distribution and select the minimal cumulative-mass prefix. **No randomness or AI model execution**.
- `pearson_correlation(left, right)`: centered, finite-binary64 numerical relationship with independent scaling and bounded work; a distinct operation from cosine similarity.
- The narrow Rust-host `scarp::native_object::emit_elf64_integer_kernel` can generate a relocatable **ELF64 x86-64 object** from a bounded sequence of dependent integer initializers. It is **not** an executable or native Scarp compile command.

Earlier alpha.4 source behavior remains separately documented. No `scarp compile`, full GPU/tensors/autodiff, stochastic sampling, native linking, package registry or stable ABI ships in alpha.5. See the [English numerical guide](/language/scientific-kernels-alpha5) and [Hindi version](/hi/language/scientific-kernels-alpha5).
